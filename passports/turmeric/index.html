03

Crop Performance

Sowing, tending and field monitoring

How the crop was tended, from field preparation onward. “Not recorded” means the information is absent from this record, not that the activity never happened.

Activity / Issue / ObservationOutcome / Impact
Grown Sunhemp prior to planting; executed deep ploughing, rotovating, bed preparation by tractor, and applied FYM at 10 Tonnes/acre.Enhanced baseline soil fertility, improved soil structure, and created well-prepared raised beds.
Planted Turmeric rhizomes on raised beds and initiated irrigation using sprinklers.Crop cycle successfully established.
Transitioned irrigation from sprinklers to drip systems; experienced brief initial moisture stress. Inter-sowed Sunhemp between beds.Good overall germination (though reduced in localized dry patches). Sunhemp suppressed weeds before being cut and mulched in situ.
Corrected plant nutrient deficiencies; introduced intercrops (Urad, Flaxseed, Wheat, Garlic). Conducted 3 earthing-up operations, hand weeding on beds, and inter-bed weeding using tractors and brushcutters.Elevated crop vigor to average levels, optimized land use, and maintained thorough weed control.
Applied prophylactic sprays of cow urine and buttermilk.Maintained organic disease management, though minor Leaf Blotch symptoms appeared in isolated patches.
Allowed plants to mature and dry naturally; left rhizomes undisturbed in the soil for 1 month of field curing.Enhanced rhizome quality, color, aroma, and storage stability.
Uncut soil and exposed rhizomes using a specialized tractor-mounted implement, followed by manual collection and field aggregation.Maximized harvest efficiency with minimal mechanical injury to rhizomes.
Executed batch processing: boiling, shade-drying, grinding fingers into powder, and final air-tight packaging.Transformed raw harvest into high-quality, market-ready packaged turmeric powder.
